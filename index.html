Say Gex Laboratory, Inc.

About Us

The Say Gex Laboratory is the brainchild of two noble conjoined twins Danil Skdelov and Brad Pit. Ironically, the Laboratory initially specialized in eugenics and genetic enhancement.

Currently under the umbrella of the megacorporation Say Gex Holdings, the Laboratory is a wholly owned subsidiary. Say Gex Holdings' Board of Directors holds absolute authority over the Laboratory's operations, personnel and research.

The story of Say Gex Laboratory and Say Gex Holdings as a whole begins on June 23rd, the day Danil and Brad were "released" from a maximum security prison. With nothing but their shared intellect and a basement beneath a children's hospital in Novokuznetsk (now the decorated HQ of Say Gex Holdings), they established their first research facility.

From these humble beginnings, the Laboratory first grew into a prominent player in the pharmaceutical and genetic engineering industries, then the science and engineering industries, eventually establishing global dominance in all industries. After a violent confrontation with the jews, they seized world government, leading to the creation of a more robust and sophisticated structure through the establishment of a higher corporate entity to oversee and govern the new industries recently added to the Laboratory's belt - Say Gex Holdings.
